{% load catalog_tags %} {% comment %} Category navigation rail for the catalogue pages. Replaces the horizontal chip rows. With nine categories and up to seven subcategories under one of them, the chips wrapped onto three lines and the parent/child relationship was carried only by a thin left rule. A vertical list has room for the full hierarchy and keeps the product grid's column count stable as the customer moves between categories. Subcategories are nested under their parent and only rendered for the ACTIVE category — the same rule the chips used, and the reason this stays short. On screens below lg the rail collapses behind a "Categories" button, so the grid keeps the full width on a phone. Bootstrap's collapse handles it; the d-lg-block on the panel keeps it permanently open on desktop regardless of the collapse state left behind by a narrow viewport. Context: the filter_* keys from _build_filter_context in portal/views.py. {% endcomment %}

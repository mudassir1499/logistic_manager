{% comment %} Product card for the PUBLIC catalogue (/shop/). Deliberately a separate partial from portal/includes/product_card.html rather than a flag on it. The two differ in the three things a card actually does: - where it links (public slug URL vs the customer portal's id URL), - what its button offers (browse/sign-in vs add-to-cart with a quantity), and - who reads it (a crawler and a stranger vs a signed-in buyer). A single template carrying all of that behind conditionals would be harder to read than two short ones, and this one has to stay easy to reason about because it is the markup Google sees. Context: data {product, first_image, cbm, in_cart} settings CompanySettings, for the currency label is_customer True when a signed-in customer is viewing {% endcomment %}

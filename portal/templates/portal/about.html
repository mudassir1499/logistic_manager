Who We Are

About Us

Your trusted China sourcing partner — we find, verify, negotiate, and deliver products from Chinese suppliers to your door.

Our Story

Bridging China and the Middle East

Founded with a mission to make China sourcing safe and simple for businesses in the UAE and wider Middle East, we have grown into a trusted purchasing partner for importers who demand quality, transparency, and reliability.

From product sourcing and supplier verification to price negotiation, quality inspection, and shipping coordination — we handle the entire procurement process on your behalf. Our team in Xuzhou, China works directly with factories, ensuring you get the best products at the best prices without ever leaving your office.

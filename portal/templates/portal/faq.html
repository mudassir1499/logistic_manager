Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about sourcing products from China with us.

We source a wide range of products including electronics, machinery, construction materials, home goods, textiles, packaging materials, auto parts, and consumer products. If you have a specific product in mind, send us the details and we will identify suitable manufacturers. We specialize in B2B bulk orders for businesses in the UAE and Middle East.

We typically charge a sourcing fee based on the complexity of the product and order size, plus a commission on the total order value. Alternatively, we can work on a fixed fee per project. All costs are agreed upfront before we begin — no hidden charges. Contact us for a detailed quote tailored to your needs.

We verify suppliers through a multi-step process: physical factory visits, business license verification, production capability audits, reference checks with past clients, and review of certifications (ISO, CE, etc.). Because our team is based in China, we can visit factories in person — something remote agents cannot do. You receive a full supplier verification report before any payment is made.

Yes. Quality inspection is one of our core services. We conduct pre-shipment inspections at the factory before goods leave China. Our inspections cover product specifications, functionality, packaging, labeling, and defect identification. We provide detailed photo and video reports so you can approve or reject the batch before shipping.

It depends on the supplier. Chinese manufacturers typically have their own MOQs, but we negotiate on your behalf to reduce minimums where possible. For the best pricing, larger quantities are recommended. We also offer order consolidation — combining multiple products from different suppliers into one shipment to save on freight costs.

Yes. We coordinate air and sea freight from China to your destination in the UAE or Middle East. We do not own cargo ships — we work with trusted freight partners to arrange the best shipping option for your order. We also prepare export documentation and support customs clearance. You get door-to-door coordination without dealing with logistics yourself.

We accept bank transfers, cash deposits, and online payments. New customers typically pay a deposit upfront to begin sourcing, with the balance due before shipping. For established clients with good payment history, we offer credit terms (Net 15 / Net 30). All invoices are available in the customer portal with downloadable PDFs and payment history.

Initial sourcing typically takes 3–7 business days depending on product complexity. After supplier selection and sample approval, production usually takes 15–45 days. Shipping adds 5–10 days by air or 25–35 days by sea to the UAE. We keep you updated at every stage through your customer portal.

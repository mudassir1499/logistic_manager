✦ 1688 Sourcing — Options

Pricing

1688 quotes a cheaper unit price at higher quantities. The extension reads the tier that applies at your order quantity and reports it as-is. No markup is added — every price is editable in the panel before you add a product, and again on the site afterwards.

Also becomes the product’s MOQ, unless the supplier’s own minimum is higher.

Product names

The page is always read directly. Names on 1688 are in Chinese, so they can optionally be translated into English before being added to your list.

Translates the product title and every variation name. No account or key needed. Prices, weights, stock and categories are always read from the page — translation never touches them.

Results are cached, so a name is only ever translated once.

Your site

Needed for “Push to site”, for category suggestions, and for the Claude extraction mode. File exports work without it.

Create one at /accounts/api-tokens/ on your site. It is shown once; if you lose it, revoke it there and make a new one.

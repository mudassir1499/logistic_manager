It used to be a standalone HTML document carrying the BACK-OFFICE styling. That made the customer journey flip themes mid-flow: someone browsing the portal (navbar, gold-on-white branding, footer) pressed Customer Login and landed on a bare centred card with none of it, then flipped back again on arrival at their dashboard. The 2FA step made it obvious because it puts a second admin-looking page in the middle of a customer's sign-in. Extending the portal base fixes the whole flow at once rather than patching the one page it was noticed on — patching only the 2FA page would have given customers TWO theme changes instead of one. It also removes three CDN dependencies. The old shell pulled Bootstrap, Bootstrap Icons and Inter from jsdelivr and fonts.googleapis.com, while the rest of the site serves those locally. Google Fonts is blocked in mainland China — where this company operates from — so the login page was the one page on the site that could render unstyled for its own staff. Staff see this too. That is deliberate: it is the public front door, and the back-office theme starts once you are actually inside it.

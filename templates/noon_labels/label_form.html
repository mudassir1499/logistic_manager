Fulfilled by Noon (FBN) Packing Guidelines
  • Individual Units: Every unit must have a scannable barcode. High-value items (e.g., electronics) must be shrink-wrapped.
  • Packaging: Ensure secure individual packaging (no loose items). Do not use noon-branded boxes for transport to the warehouse.
  • Cartons: Segregate by Partner SKU (PSKU). Do not mix ASNs in the same carton. Use gap filling materials like bubble wrap.
  • Labeling: Every carton must visibly display its number (e.g., CARTON 1), ASN, PSKU, and total product quantity.
  • Documentation: Bring two printed copies of your ASN (Advanced Shipment Notice) when delivering to the warehouse.

How this works
  1. Create the quotation here, then add priced lines — catalogue products or free text for anything not in the catalogue yet.
  2. Mark it sent when it goes to the customer. That locks the lines, so what they are holding cannot change underneath them.
  3. Record the outcome: accepted, declined (with the reason) or expired. The reason is what makes the conversion rate worth reading.
  4. An accepted quotation converts into an order, carrying its lines across.
